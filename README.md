# Human Resources Internship — Sanyukt Organisation

## Overview

This repository showcases the work I completed during my **Human Resources (HR) Internship at Sanyukt Organisation**.

It documents my internship assignments, research reports, case study analysis, recruitment materials, visual communication deliverables, and reflections on practical HR learning.

The purpose of this repository is to maintain a professional portfolio of my completed work and demonstrate my understanding of human resource management concepts and practices.

## Internship Details

- **Organisation:** Sanyukt Organisation
- **Department:** Human Resources
- **Internship Status:** Completed
- **Portfolio Type:** HR Internship Projects and Deliverables

## Tasks & Deliverables

### 1. Organisation Introduction
A presentation introducing the organisation and its work.

### 2. Job Descriptions & Recruitment Materials
Preparation of job descriptions and recruitment posters for Marketing Intern and Content Writer Intern opportunities.

### 3. HR Research Report
Research on the latest trends in Human Resource Management, including artificial intelligence in recruitment, people analytics, hybrid work, employee well-being, skills-based hiring, and digital HR transformation.

### 4. HR Case Study — Google's Project Oxygen
Analysis of Google's data-driven approach to leadership development, manager effectiveness, employee feedback, and people analytics.

### 5. HR Marketing & Employer Branding Report
A summary of LinkedIn Talent Solutions' *The Future of Recruiting 2025*, covering AI in recruitment, quality of hire, employer branding, and skills-based hiring.

### 6. Recruitment-to-Onboarding Infographic
A visual overview of the recruitment process, covering sourcing, screening, interviewing, selection and offer, and onboarding.

### 7. Social Media Promotion
An assignment focused on promoting the organisation's social media presence.

### 8. Candidate Outreach
Practical exposure to candidate communication and outreach for an open opportunity.

### 9. HR Analytics Article
An article exploring how HR analytics can support employee and volunteer retention in non-profit organisations.

### 10. Internship Reflection
A reflection on completed assignments, key learnings, challenges, and suggestions for improving the internship experience.
